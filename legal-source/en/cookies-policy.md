# COOKIES POLICY — Myble (my-ble.eu)

**Version 1.1 — Effective date: 27 June 2026**

## 1\. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device that help the website
function and may enable analytics and advertising.

## 2\. Categories of cookies we use

  - **Strictly necessary cookies (always on):** core site functions such
    as the shopping cart, session, security and storing your
    cookie-consent choice. These do not require consent.
  - **Analytics cookies (optional):** product analytics and usage
    measurement to improve the site (PostHog). Loaded **only** after
    consent.
  - **Marketing cookies (optional):** advertising measurement and
    remarketing (Google Ads). Loaded **only** after consent.

## 3\. Consent and control

We ask for consent for non-essential cookies via a **cookie banner**
before any such cookie is set. You can **change or withdraw** consent at
any time via the cookie settings on the website. Refusing optional
cookies does not affect access to the E-shop.
