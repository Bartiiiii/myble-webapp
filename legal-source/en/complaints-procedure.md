# COMPLAINTS PROCEDURE (REKLAMAČNÍ ŘÁD) — Myble (my-ble.eu)

**Version 1.0 (MVP) — Effective date: 27 June 2026**

## 1\. Scope

1.1. This Complaints Procedure governs the exercise of rights from
defective performance (“complaints”) for Goods bought from the Seller
(Bartłomiej Karol Kwaśnica, IČO 24439673) via my-ble.eu, and complies
with the Czech Civil Code and the Consumer Protection Act. 1.2. For
consumers, nothing in this Procedure limits mandatory statutory rights.

## 2\. The Seller’s liability for defects

2.1. The Seller is liable that the Goods are free of defects on receipt
and conform to the contract (quantity, dimensions ordered, quality,
purpose). 2.2. A consumer may exercise rights from defective performance
for **defects that appear within 24 months of receipt.** 2.3. Liability
does not cover: normal wear and tear; defects caused by incorrect
assembly contrary to the instructions; improper use, overloading, or
storage in unsuitable conditions (e.g. excessive humidity, direct heat);
or damage caused by the consumer after receipt.

## 3\. Manufacturing tolerances

3.1. Dimensional deviations within normal manufacturing tolerances
(typically millimetre level) and reasonable variations in décor/shade
are not defects.

## 4\. How to file a complaint

4.1. Submit the complaint **without undue delay** after discovering the
defect to **myble.eu@gmail.com**, including: - order number, -
description of the defect (and how it appears), - photo/video
documentation, - preferred remedy (if any). 4.2. For **visible transport
damage**, document it on receipt (photos of packaging and Goods) and
notify the Seller without undue delay. 4.3. The Seller confirms receipt
of the complaint and the date it was filed.

## 5\. Remedies

5.1. Depending on the nature of the defect and statutory rules, the
consumer may be entitled to: **repair**, **replacement** (of the Goods
or a part), a **reasonable discount**, or **withdrawal** from the
contract where the defect is material or repair/replacement is not
possible/timely. 5.2. The Seller may supply replacement parts (e.g. a
single panel or fitting) where this remedies the defect.

## 6\. Handling time

6.1. The Seller will **decide on the complaint without undue delay** (in
complex cases within 3 working days), and will **handle the complaint,
including any repair, within 30 days** of the date it was filed, unless
a longer period is agreed with the consumer. 6.2. If the 30-day period
is exceeded without agreement, the consumer has the rights that arise
from a material breach of contract. 6.3. The Seller issues the consumer
a written confirmation of when the complaint was filed, its content, the
requested remedy, and subsequently of the date and manner of resolution.

## 7\. Costs

7.1. Where a complaint is justified, the consumer is entitled to
reimbursement of the **reasonable costs** incurred in exercising the
right (e.g. return postage).

## 8\. Out-of-court dispute resolution

8.1. A consumer may turn to the **Czech Trade Inspection Authority
(Česká obchodní inspekce, ČOI), coi.gov.cz**, for out-of-court
resolution of a consumer dispute.
