## 3\. Materials

Furniture is made from **melamine-faced chipboard (laminated
particleboard, MFC)** with matching **ABS edge banding**, in the
following décors and thicknesses: - **Kronospan U8685 BS “Biel
Alpejska”** (white) — 18 mm - **EGGER U999 ST7 “Czarny”** (black) — 18
mm - **Kronospan WU8685 BS** (white) — 36 mm - **EGGER U999 ST7
“Czarny”** (black) — 36 mm

Each piece is custom-made, so the exact board, thickness and dimensions
depend on the configuration ordered.

## 4\. Safety information shown before purchase (website)

  - The product is supplied as a **flat-pack kit for self-assembly**.
  - **Anti-tip / stability warning:** tall or top-heavy units **must be
    anchored to the wall** using suitable fixings. There is a risk of
    tip-over and serious injury, especially to children, if a unit is
    not anchored.
  - Intended for **normal indoor, domestic use** only; not for outdoor
    or high-humidity environments.
  - **Load capacity** depends on the configuration (shelf span, board
    thickness, fixings). As general guidance for evenly distributed
    loads, **18 mm** shelves are intended for light-to-medium loads and
    **36 mm** shelves for heavier loads. **Do not exceed the load limit
    stated for your specific configuration** (shown on the product page
    / in the assembly manual). Always distribute weight evenly.
  - Keep small parts (screws, fittings) away from children during
    assembly — choking hazard.

## 5\. In-box assembly & safety sheet

Each kit includes (printed and/or via QR to a digital version): 1.
**Parts list** with quantities and part numbers. 2. **Tools required.**
3. **Step-by-step assembly instructions** (diagrams). 4.
**Wall-anchoring instructions** with the anti-tip warning shown
prominently, including guidance to use fixings appropriate to the wall
type. 5. **Load limit** and usage limits for the configuration. 6.
**Care & maintenance** (cleaning; avoiding humidity and direct heat). 7.
**Safety warnings** (do not climb/stand on the furniture; keep children
away during assembly). 8. **Manufacturer contact** for questions and
spare parts (Section 1).

## 6\. Incident handling

6.1. The manufacturer keeps a log of any reported safety incident or
injury. 6.2. Where a product presents a risk, the manufacturer will take
corrective action (warning, repair, recall) and, where required, inform
the relevant authority via the EU **Safety Business Gateway**.
