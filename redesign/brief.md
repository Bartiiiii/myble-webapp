# Myble Redesign — Consolidated Brief (one page)

**From:** Mara (Marketing Lead) · **Date:** 2026-06-21 · **Inputs:** [audit.md](audit.md), [research.md](research.md), Target Customer Deep Dive

## The problem in one line
We built a **maker's design tool in English**; our customer is a **Czech homeowner with a 73 cm alcove** who wants it solved, priced instantly, and trusted. The gap is total — positioning, language, products, configurator, and trust all point the wrong way.

## What we're changing (the 6 bets)
1. **Problem-led, CZ-first hero.** Lead with the awkward space and "fits to the millimetre," in Czech, with CZK. Kill "Create your own furniture / Figma for furniture / Community."
2. **Right products.** Shelves, alcove units, side tables, slim wardrobes (parcel-rule, flat-pack). Cut sofas/chairs/dining tables.
3. **Presets-first configurator with an always-on instant price.** Enter your space in cm → curated decors → live CZK price → validation. No raw X/Y/Z CAD.
4. **A real trust system** (the conversion lever): reviews/Trustpilot + real install photos, swatch samples (99 Kč, credited), measuring guide, courier-proof packaging, honest defects-only returns, clear lead time.
5. **Pricing transparency everywhere** — "od 2 490 Kč" on home; honest checkout breakdown; dimension-confirmation step before pay.
6. **Premium-minimal brand discipline** — white/zinc/indigo/oak, big whitespace, soft shadows, one accent, calm motion. No loud gradients, no rustic brown 3D.

## Who we serve (and ignore)
- **Primary:** Tereza (niche-filler), Jakub & Lucie (new-build family). **Secondary:** Marek (home-office nook), Tomasz (DIY/PL beachhead).
- **Ignore:** bargain hunters, full-bespoke luxury, won't-assemble, oversized/wall-to-wall seekers.

## The competitive wedge (from research)
Own the square no one holds: **Czech-first, premium-minimal, problem-led, instant-price storefront for small made-to-measure pieces**, with trust as a first-class system. Match Tylko's "to-the-cm" + instant price; beat it on Czech locality, simpler/cheaper pieces, and damage-proof + honest returns (its top complaints). Beat CZ incumbents (AMONIT/Komandor/joiners) who gate price behind consultations.

## Definition of done (unchanged)
Every persona says *"clearly for me, and I trust it."* Mobile-first, WCAG AA, fast, on-brand; configurator still works. Deliverables in `redesign/`.

## The one strategic choice for the founder (Phase 2 gate)
**Direction A — "Calm Utility" (recommended):** Stripe/Linear-clean, trust-forward, the configurator and instant price front-and-centre. Lowest risk, maximal "I trust it."
**Direction B — "Editorial Warmth":** more lifestyle-led, big before/after room photography, magazine type. Warmer, more aspirational, slightly higher production cost (needs real photography).
→ Both share the same IA, tokens, and copy; they differ in hero treatment and imagery weight. **Recommendation: ship A's structure with a touch of B's lifestyle proof.**
